YouTube subscriptions feed

A temporary test of YouTube.js authentication and feed pagination. This page sends your cookie directly to the Kairo staging Worker over HTTPS. It does not save it in browser storage.

Use only a separate test account. A YouTube cookie is a session credential. Never use this on production, public/shared devices, or an untrusted staging domain. Clear the form after each test. This uses YouTube.js and YouTube's undocumented InnerTube interface.

The cookie is sent only in the HTTPS request body, never in the URL. The Worker is configured not to persist or log it. The form clears the cookie field after the request.
